## Trailhead 1.0.0

First release.

- Continent scale world generation with two climate layouts: Latitude (north is cold, south is warm) and Climate Zones
- 16 landscape regions modelled on real places, including the Dolomites, Tetons, Pacific Northwest, Zion canyons, fjords, Atlantic sea cliffs, steppe, savanna, deserts, jungle, boreal forest, tundra and ice sheets
- Rivers that drain downhill to the sea, with lakes, waterfalls and plunge pools
- Mountain ranges that grow out of foothills, with rain shadows behind them
- Oceans with continental shelves, volcanic islands, atolls and rocky archipelagos
- Caves built on vanilla's cave noise, shaped by local geology, with groundwater lakes, ore veins, lush, dripstone and deep dark caves, and new desert and ice caves
- 31 custom biomes with region-specific trees, ground cover and rock
- Vanilla structures, including villages, ancient cities, trial chambers and mineshafts
